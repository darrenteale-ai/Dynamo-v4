1. Introduction

Dynamo is where we record, investigate and learn from incidents, near misses, complaints and safety concerns. It also keeps track of our legal reporting duties (duty of candour, RIDDOR and CQC notifications) and runs vehicle daily checks.

There are two ways people use Dynamo:

Who How Login needed?
Crew and staff on shift Scan a QR code to report an incident or complete a vehicle daily check No
Managers and admins Sign in to Dynamo to review, investigate and close incidents, and run fleet, staff and settings Yes

Only manager and admin accounts can sign in. Crew never need an account.

Signing in

  1. Go to the Dynamo web address and enter your work email and password.
  2. Your session lasts 12 hours, then you'll need to sign in again.
  3. After 5 wrong passwords your account is locked for 15 minutes. If you've forgotten your password, ask an admin to reset it (see section 10).

Finding your way around

The sidebar on the left has these pages:

  • Dashboard — a summary of incidents, deadlines and trends.
  • Incident Reporting — the list of all incidents. This is where most work happens.
  • Corrective Actions — every open action from every incident, with owners and due dates.
  • Safety Alerts — alerts raised from incidents, which staff must read and acknowledge.
  • Fleet Management — vehicles, key dates, daily checks and defects.
  • Staff — manager and admin accounts.
  • Case Notes — running notes on ongoing matters.
  • Settings — your details, password, deadlines and admin tools.

A Privacy notice link appears at the bottom of every page.

2. Reporting an incident (crew — QR form)

Every vehicle and base has a QR code that opens the incident report form. No login is needed. A manager can show the code on screen from Incident Reporting → Show QR code.

Filling in the form

  1. Name of person reporting — your name.
  2. Date and time of the incident (defaults to now — change it if the incident was earlier).
  3. Vehicle registration and call sign.
  4. CAD Ref, if there is one.
  5. Incident type — Clinical, Medication, Vehicle Damage, Safeguarding, Complaint, Near Miss, Violence or Infection Control.
  6. Was anyone hurt or injured, or is this a safeguarding concern? — answer honestly. If you answer Yes, managers are alerted the moment you submit.
  7. Crew members — names and grades of both crew.
  8. Email address — so you get a confirmation with your reference number.
  9. Was the on-call manager informed? — if yes, their name and the time you called.
  10. Location — Luton Base, Stevenage or Southall.
  11. Incident description — what happened, in your own words. Stick to facts.
  12. Photos (optional) — up to 6, for example vehicle damage or the scene. They're shrunk automatically so they upload on a weak signal.

Press Submit report. You'll see a reference number like 13/11/2026 - 0004. Note it down and quote it in any follow-up.

If anyone is in immediate danger, call 999 first. The form is for recording, not for emergencies.

Anonymous near-miss reports

If you choose Near Miss, you can tick Report this near miss anonymously. Your email won't be recorded and no confirmation is sent. Anonymous reporting is only available for near misses.

What happens next

  • The report goes straight into Dynamo as OPEN with severity LOW. A manager sets the real severity when they review it.
  • Managers are emailed immediately if you said someone was hurt, or if the type is Safeguarding or Violence.
  • A manager must be allocated to the incident within the allocation window (24 hours by default).

3. Reporting an incident (managers — inside Dynamo)

Managers can also log incidents directly:

  1. Go to Incident Reporting and click + Report incident.
  2. Fill in the date, time, location, category, severity and what happened. CAD ref, call sign, crew names and people involved are optional.
  3. Choose a Responsible manager (allocate now) to allocate the incident straight away, or leave it as Allocate later.
  4. Click save. Choosing HIGH or CRITICAL severity emails all managers and admins straight away.

4. Managing incidents

The incident list

Incident Reporting shows every incident, newest first. Each row shows:

  • Severity — LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH or CRITICAL.
  • Status — OPEN, INVESTIGATING or CLOSED, plus amber or red badges for anything regulatory still to do (for example Candour: decide, RIDDOR: to submit, CQC: overdue).
  • Response — how close the incident is to its response deadline: On track, Due soon (within 24 hours) or Overdue.
  • Allocation — the responsible manager's name once someone is allocated; until then, whether allocation is on track, due soon or overdue.
  • Investigator — the investigator named in the sign-off section, once entered.

Filters let you narrow the list by search text, status, severity, category, responsible manager, date range, and these tick-boxes:

  • RIDDOR / CQC — incidents flagged for either.
  • Unallocated overdue — no manager assigned and the allocation deadline has passed.
  • Candour / RIDDOR / CQC action due — anything with a regulatory step still outstanding.

Deadlines on every incident

Deadline Default Measured from What happens if missed
Allocate a responsible manager 24 hours When the report arrives Shown red; banner on the incident
Respond to the incident 5 days Date of the incident Shown red; escalation email to managers and admins 24 hours after it goes overdue

Admins can change both in Settings.

Opening an incident

Click any row to open it. At the top you'll see the details from the report, then these sections:

  • Photos & statements — files attached to the incident.
  • Corrective actions — what we're doing to stop it happening again.
  • Safety alert — raise an alert for all staff.
  • Risk assessment — likelihood × impact matrix.
  • Investigation — reference details — responsible manager, CAD ref, crew, on-call manager, key dates, related incident.
  • Harm and duty of candour.
  • Regulatory reporting — RIDDOR and CQC.
  • Investigation — write-up — outcome, recommendations, lessons learnt.
  • Sign-off.
  • Status and Admin notes — directly under Sign-off, so you can sign off and close in one place.
  • Audit trail.

Click Save changes at the bottom to save everything in one go.

Every time you open an incident, your name is recorded in the audit trail, even if you only read it. Only open incidents you have a reason to look at.

Allocating a responsible manager

In Investigation — reference details, choose the Responsible manager and save. Do this within the allocation window.

Setting severity with the risk matrix

In Risk assessment, pick a Likelihood (Rare to Very likely) and an Impact (Trivial to Extreme). Dynamo suggests a severity from the matrix. You can override it by choosing Severity directly. Raising severity to HIGH or CRITICAL emails all managers and admins.

Status

Status Meaning
OPEN Reported, not yet being investigated
INVESTIGATING A manager is working on it
CLOSED Finished and signed off. The incident becomes read-only.

Linking related incidents

If two reports describe the same event, or one follows from another, choose it under Related / duplicate incident.

5. Harm and duty of candour

The duty of candour (CQC Regulation 20) is a legal duty to be open with people when something goes wrong in their care and causes moderate harm or worse. Dynamo records each step so we can show CQC we did it.

Step 1 — Record the harm level

In Harm and duty of candour, choose the Harm level:

  • No harm
  • Low harm
  • Moderate harm
  • Severe harm
  • Prolonged psychological harm
  • Death

Every incident needs a harm level before it can be closed. Choose No harm if nobody was harmed.

Step 2 — Decide whether it is a notifiable safety incident

If the harm is moderate or worse, Dynamo asks Notifiable safety incident? and the incident shows a Candour: decide badge until you answer.

It is notifiable if all of these are true:

  • it was unintended or unexpected;
  • it happened while we were providing care; and
  • in a clinician's reasonable opinion it caused, or could result in, moderate harm, severe harm, prolonged psychological harm or death.

Choose Yes — duty of candour applies or No and save. Dynamo records who made the decision and when. If you're unsure, ask the registered manager or clinical lead before deciding.

Step 3 — Complete the candour steps

When the answer is Yes, record each step as you do it:

Step What to record
Tell the person (or their family) in person Date person told in person
Apologise Tick An apology was given
Follow up in writing Date written follow-up sent, and upload a copy of the letter under Photos & statements
Share the findings Date findings shared, once the investigation is complete

Use Candour notes to record who was told and what support was offered.

A Progress badge shows whether you're on track against the candour target (10 days from the decision by default, set by admins in Settings). The law says "as soon as reasonably practicable", so treat the target as a maximum, not a goal.

Saying sorry is not admitting fault.

6. RIDDOR and CQC reporting

Both live in the Regulatory reporting section of an incident.

RIDDOR (Health and Safety Executive)

  1. Tick Reportable under RIDDOR (to HSE).
  2. Choose the RIDDOR report type. This sets the deadline:
Type Deadline (from the date of the incident)
Work-related death 10 days — and notify HSE without delay
Specified injury to a worker 10 days — and notify HSE without delay
Over-7-day incapacitation of a worker 15 days
Non-worker taken to hospital for treatment 10 days
Dangerous occurrence 10 days
  1. Submit the report on the HSE website, then record the Date submitted to HSE and the HSE reference.

A badge shows the deadline: On track, Due soon (within 3 days), Overdue or Submitted. For over-7-day injuries, remember the 15 days run from the day of the accident, not from day 7.

CQC notifications

  1. Tick Notifiable to CQC. The clock starts the moment you save.
  2. Choose the CQC notification type: death, serious injury, abuse or allegation of abuse, police incident, event that stops the service running safely, or other.
  3. Send the notification to CQC, then record the Date notification sent and any CQC reference.

CQC notifications must be sent without delay. Dynamo shows Due soon after 12 hours and Overdue after 24 hours.

Deadline emails

Managers and admins are emailed when a candour, RIDDOR or CQC item is due soon, again if it becomes overdue, and when a candour decision is needed. Each alert is sent once per incident.

7. Investigating and closing an incident

Writing up the investigation

In Investigation — write-up, record:

  • Outcome of investigation — what you found.
  • Recommendations / conclusions.
  • Lessons learnt / action plan.

Add corrective actions for anything that needs doing (see section 8).

Sign-off (mandatory)

All four sign-off fields must be completed before an incident can be closed:

  • Signature (typed name)
  • Investigator name
  • Designation — for example Operations Manager
  • Date

Closing

Set Status to CLOSED. If anything is missing, a Before this incident can be closed checklist appears and the empty sign-off fields are outlined in red. Dynamo won't close the incident until everything on the list is done:

  • harm level recorded;
  • candour decision made, if harm is moderate or worse;
  • all four candour steps recorded, if it is a notifiable safety incident;
  • RIDDOR type, date submitted and HSE reference, if RIDDOR reportable;
  • CQC notification type and date sent, if CQC notifiable;
  • all four sign-off fields.

Once closed, the incident is read-only. It can't be edited or reopened. If new information comes to light, log a new incident and link it using Related / duplicate incident.

8. Photos, actions, alerts, audit trail and exports

Photos and statements

In Photos & statements, use Upload photo or Upload statement (up to 20MB each). Click a file to open it, or rename it. Opening a file is recorded in the audit trail. Files can't be added once the incident is closed.

Corrective actions

  1. In the incident, enter the action, choose an Owner and a Due date, and click Add action.
  2. Move it through Open → In progress → Done as work progresses.
  3. The Corrective Actions page shows overdue actions across all incidents; click Show all open actions to see the rest. Overdue actions also appear on the Dashboard and in the weekly summary.

Safety alerts

Use a safety alert when every manager needs to know something learned from an incident.

  1. In the incident, click Raise a safety alert from this incident, give it a title and message, and save.
  2. Everyone sees it on the Safety Alerts page and clicks to acknowledge they've read it.
  3. Click Who's seen it? to see who has and hasn't acknowledged. Archive an alert when it's no longer needed.

The audit trail

Every incident keeps a permanent record, with names and times, of:

  • who reported it and every change of status, severity and responsible manager;
  • each harm level, candour, RIDDOR and CQC step;
  • corrective actions and safety alerts;
  • every PDF export, and every summary report or subject access export the incident was included in;
  • every staff member who opened the incident, and every photo or statement opened.

An Opened by line above the trail summarises who has viewed the incident, how many times and when they last opened it. Every open is recorded; the automatic refresh after you save or upload a file is not counted as a new visit.

Exporting

  • Single incident — Download as PDF (Investigation form) in an incident downloads a PDF (form QA4) with all details, the investigation, sign-off, the regulatory record, photos and the audit trail. Each export is logged.
  • Summary report — Incident Reporting → Export summary downloads a Word summary for a date range, broken down by category, severity, status, vehicle and location, with overdue incidents and RIDDOR/CQC flags.

9. Dashboard, case notes and fleet

Dashboard

At-a-glance figures: total, open and overdue incidents, this month's count, RIDDOR and CQC flags, overdue actions and unread safety alerts. Charts show incidents per week and by category, severity, status, response status, vehicle and location, plus a list of incidents with regulatory flags or outstanding actions.

Case notes

For ongoing matters that need a running record. Click + New note to start one, then + Add to add dated entries. Each entry records who wrote it.

Fleet management

Fleet Management has three tabs:

  • Vehicles — registration, call sign, type, status (Available, Off road, Maintenance) and due dates for MOT, service, insurance and tax. Dates turn amber within 30 days and red when overdue. Use + Add vehicle to add one.
  • Daily checks — every vehicle daily inspection, with who checked it, mileage and the result.
  • Defects — problems found in checks or added by managers. Click Resolve with a note when fixed, or Reopen if needed.

Print QR codes prints one code per vehicle, plus a general one.

Vehicle daily inspection (crew)

At the start of every shift, crew scan the vehicle's QR code (no login):

  1. Check the vehicle and your name. Enter the mileage.
  2. Answer every item OK or Problem — exterior, mechanical, and cab and saloon (including oxygen, defibrillator and drug bags).
  3. For any problem, describe it. Each problem becomes a defect and managers are emailed.
  4. Submit.

10. Admin tasks

Staff accounts

On the Staff page, admins and managers can + Add staff, change a person's name, phone and role (Manager or Admin), and deactivate leavers. Deactivating an account signs the person out immediately.

Settings

  • User guide — opens this guide.
  • My details and Change password — for everyone.
  • Password reset — select staff and generate temporary passwords. Give each person their temporary password securely and ask them to change it after signing in.
  • Incident response SLA — days to respond (1–5), hours to allocate a manager, and the duty of candour target.
  • Weekly summary email — click Send weekly digest now to email the summary of open, overdue and regulatory items to all managers and admins.

Emails Dynamo sends

Email Sent to When
Report received The reporter When a QR report with an email is submitted
URGENT: incident reported via QR form Managers and admins Reporter said someone was hurt, or Safeguarding / Violence
HIGH/CRITICAL severity incident Managers and admins Logged at, or raised to, HIGH or CRITICAL
ESCALATION: response overdue Managers and admins 24 hours after the response deadline passes
Regulatory deadlines need action Managers and admins Candour, RIDDOR or CQC due soon or overdue, or candour decision needed
Vehicle defect reported Managers and admins A daily check finds a problem
Weekly summary Managers and admins When sent from Settings

Alert emails never include the incident description, because it may contain patient information. Open the incident in Dynamo to read it. If emails aren't arriving, ask an admin to check that email sending is set up.

Data protection (admins)

The Data protection tools page (/gdpr) lets admins:

  • find everything held about a person and download a subject access export (check and redact it before sending);
  • anonymise a person following an erasure request;
  • set retention periods, preview and run retention;
  • view the log of every data protection action.

The Privacy notice (/privacy) explains to staff and the public what Dynamo holds and why.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I close an incident? Set the status to Closed and read the checklist that appears. Everything listed must be done first.

Can I reopen a closed incident? No. Log a new incident and link it with Related / duplicate incident.

I've forgotten my password. Ask an admin to issue a temporary password from Settings → Password reset.

The incident says "Candour: decide". What do I do? The harm level is moderate or worse. Decide whether it's a notifiable safety incident (section 5) and save.

Who can see that I opened an incident? Anyone who can open the incident can see the audit trail, including the Opened by list.

A crew member reported something serious through the QR form but it shows as LOW. QR reports always start as LOW. Review it, set the severity and harm level, and allocate a manager.
